How this demo calculates fees
  • Buyer service fee: 7% of item subtotal.
  • Seller fee: 10% of item subtotal.
  • Demo tax: 13% of subtotal, delivery, and buyer fee.
  • The full tip is added to the seller payout.
  • These rules are fictional and not financial guidance.

Design principle

Disclosure belongs before commitment.

A fee is not transparent because it exists somewhere in a help centre. People need the amount, recipient, and reason at the moment it affects a decision.
